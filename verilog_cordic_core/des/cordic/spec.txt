## 7. PDF 依据摘要（已提炼）
- CORDIC (Coordinate Rotation Digital Computer) is an algorithm for computing transcendental functions like sine, cosine and arctangent.
- In vector mode the inputs are sin/cos and the computer returns the arctangent.
- The constant K means that the CORDIC function as an overall gain by the factor 1/K which is often referred to as the CORDIC gain.
- There are also provisions for handling arbitrary bit lengths and iterations.
