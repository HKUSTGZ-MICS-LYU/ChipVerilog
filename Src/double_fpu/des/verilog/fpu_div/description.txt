fpu_div is the intermediate datapath for IEEE-754 double-precision division. It takes two 64-bit operands, `opa` as the dividend and `opb` as the divisor, and produces a sign bit, a 12-bit intermediate exponent, and a 56-bit extended mantissa bundle for a later rounding and packing stage.

The module does not generate the final 64-bit IEEE-754 result. Special cases such as NaN, infinity, divide-by-zero, invalid operations, overflow, and final rounding are expected to be handled outside this module.

The result sign is computed as `opa[63] ^ opb[63]`. The operand mantissas are expanded into internal 54-bit significands. Normalized operands use an inserted implicit leading 1, while denormalized operands are shifted according to leading-zero detection.

Division is performed iteratively using a long-division style compare/subtract/shift loop. On each active iteration, the current dividend is compared with the divisor to generate one quotient bit. If the dividend is large enough, the divisor is subtracted and the result is shifted left; otherwise, the dividend is shifted left unchanged. The divisor remains fixed during the iteration loop, and the counter is initialized from `preset = 53`.

The exponent datapath computes the biased division exponent and applies correction for operand normalization, denormal inputs, and denormal result alignment. If operand A is zero, `exponent_out` is forced to zero.

`mantissa_7` is an intermediate rounding bundle: {reserved bit, leading-bit indicator, 52-bit mantissa, guard-like bit, sticky bit}

The sticky bit is generated by OR-reducing the lower aligned remainder bits. Because the module has no `valid` or `done` output, external control logic must track the operation latency and keep operands properly aligned with the outputs.

Processing Flow:
1. The module extracts the sign, exponent, and fraction fields from opa and opb.
2. The result sign is computed as opa[63] ^ opb[63].
3. The operand fraction fields are expanded into internal 54-bit significands. For normalized operands, the hidden leading 1 is inserted. For denormalized operands, leading-zero detection determines the left shift needed before forming the internal significand.
4. The internal dividend and divisor are loaded after the enable signal passes through the module’s enable pipeline.
5. Division is performed using an iterative binary long-division process. The divisor remains fixed during the divide loop. On each active iteration, the current dividend register is compared with the divisor register. If the dividend is greater than or equal to the divisor, the selected quotient bit is set, the divisor is subtracted, and the difference is shifted left. If the dividend is smaller than the divisor, the selected quotient bit is cleared and the dividend is shifted left unchanged.
6. The loop counter is initialized from preset = 53 and counts down during quotient generation.
7. The exponent datapath forms an initial biased division exponent using:exponent_a + 1023 - exponent_b,It then applies corrections for denormalized operands, quotient normalization, and possible denormal-result alignment.
8. If operand A is zero, exponent_out is forced to zero.
