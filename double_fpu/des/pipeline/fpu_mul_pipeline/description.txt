Implementation:
- The module computes double-precision floating-point multiplication by forming the significand product, adjusting exponent/sign, and normalizing the result path.
- rounding will be performed, with the least significant remainder bit calculated by performing an OR on any bits that were shifted out of the mantissa due to the difference in exponents.
- The multiplication operation is performed in the module (fpu_mul) in the source file, (fpu_mul.v).
- The mantissa of operand A and the leading '1' (for normalized numbers) are stored in the 53-bit register (mul_a).
- The mantissa of operand B and the leading '1' (for normalized numbers) are stored in the 53-bit register (mul_b).
