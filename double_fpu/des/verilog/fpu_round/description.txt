Module `fpu_round` applies IEEE-754 rounding-mode rules to intermediate mantissa/exponent values and produces the final rounded data word.
fpu_round.v receives the intermediate floating-point result from the arithmetic stage, decodes the selected rounding mode, decides whether the retained mantissa must be incremented, propagates a carry from the rounding increment into the exponent when necessary, and packs the rounded sign, exponent, and mantissa into a 64-bit floating-point result for the exception stage.
This module applies the selected rounding rule to an intermediate floating-point result. It accepts a sign bit, a 56-bit mantissa bundle, and a 12-bit exponent. It may increment the mantissa, renormalize if the increment overflows, update the exponent accordingly, and then emits a rounded IEEE-754 64-bit result plus the final 12-bit exponent.

Description:

This module performs the rounding stage of the floating-point datapath. Its inputs are sign_term, mantissa_term, and exponent_term, which come from the previous arithmetic stage. mantissa_term is 56 bits wide and contains an extra MSB, a leading bit, the 52-bit mantissa, and 2 extra remainder bits. exponent_term is a 12-bit extended exponent whose top bit is used to catch exponent overflow and prevent rollover that would occur with only 11 bits. The outputs are the final rounded 64-bit value round_out and the rounded exponent exponent_final, which is passed on to the exceptions stage .

The module supports four rounding modes: round to nearest, round to zero, round to positive infinity, and round to negative infinity. For round-to-nearest mode, rounding is triggered if the first extra remainder bit is 1 and the least significant mantissa bit is 1. Rounding is performed by adding rounding_amount to mantissa_term, where rounding_amount has a single 1 aligned with the least significant mantissa bit. For round-to-zero mode, no active increment is performed, so the result is effectively truncated. For round-to-positive-infinity mode, the result is incremented if the sign is positive and either remainder bit is 1. For round-to-negative-infinity mode, the result is incremented if the sign is negative and either remainder bit is 1 .

If rounding causes the mantissa to overflow, the rounded value is shifted right by one and the exponent is incremented. Otherwise, the exponent remains unchanged. The final round_out consists of the result sign, the rounded 11-bit exponent, and the rounded 52-bit mantissa. This module only performs normal rounding. It does not resolve infinity, NaN, overflow/underflow replacement, or exception flags; those are handled in the exceptions module.

All intermediate registers and outputs are updated on the rising edge of the clock. When rst is asserted, all rounding-related registers and outputs are cleared.
