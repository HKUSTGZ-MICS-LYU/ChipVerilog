## 2. Functional Requirements
- Functional intent: Module `i2c_master_byte_ctrl` sequences byte-level I2C transfers by issuing bit-controller commands, shifting data, and handling ACK/NACK phases.
- SHALL: The implementation SHALL preserve fundamental I2C timing: SDA transitions only while SCL is low, except START/STOP conditions.
- SHALL: Command/status semantics SHALL remain consistent with register definitions (e.g., STA/STO/RD/WR/ACK/IACK, TIP, IF, AL).
- SHALL: Wishbone-side interaction SHALL keep deterministic `ack` timing and atomic register read/write behavior.
- SHALL: The byte-level controller SHOULD orchestrate 8-bit transfers, ACK sampling, and command-complete handshake.
